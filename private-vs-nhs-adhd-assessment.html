Private vs NHS ADHD assessment in the UK

Both routes can lead to a clinical assessment. The practical differences are usually about access, waiting times, cost, provider arrangements and what happens after diagnosis.

NHS assessment routes

NHS pathways vary by area and eligibility. A GP can explain the local referral route. In England, some people may also be eligible to choose an NHS-contracted provider through patient choice arrangements, depending on the service and circumstances.

Private assessment

A private assessment is paid for directly by the patient and may offer faster access. Before booking, check who will assess you, the clinician’s professional registration and ADHD experience, what the fee includes, how the report is provided and what options exist after diagnosis.

Medication after diagnosis

A diagnosis and medication treatment are separate stages. If medication is being considered, ask in advance who would initiate and monitor treatment, what the additional costs are, and what happens if a GP does not accept shared care.

Do not choose on speed alone

Waiting time matters, but so do clinical quality, transparency, continuity and the usefulness of the report afterwards. A good provider should explain its process clearly before you pay.

Questions worth asking

Ask about the clinician, assessment method, supporting information, report timescale, total fees, cancellation terms, prescribing arrangements and how the provider communicates with your GP or other professionals where appropriate.
