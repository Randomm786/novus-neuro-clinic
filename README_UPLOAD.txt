3. index.html, the other HTML files, CNAME, sitemap.xml and assets/ must remain

4. Include the new adhd-treatment-and-titration.html page.

   They are not server-side HTTP 301 redirects.
6. Keep the existing Pages/custom-domain settings. The included CNAME is unchanged.
7. After deployment, check the homepage, adult assessment, Treatment, contact and
   both old assessment URLs. This package has not been deployed to your repository.

FORM

must send that draft. It does not store clinical records, confirm delivery, take
payment or record a completed assessment. Do not collect medical records here.
