Founders' vision

A new benchmark for accessible, specialist and outcome-focused care.

Novus exists to improve access to high-quality neurodevelopmental care through clinically robust, evidence-based ADHD and autism services that are inclusive, well governed and focused on meaningful long-term outcomes.

What Novus means

New — but grounded.

The name Novus comes from Latin and means new. It reflects an ambition to establish a new standard for neurodevelopmental healthcare by combining specialist medical expertise, multidisciplinary care, cultural accessibility, robust governance and meaningful long-term support.

Our vision is not simply to diagnose ADHD and autism.It is to help people understand their needs, improve their health, reach their potential and thrive.
NOVUS

Five principles behind the clinic.

N

Neurodiversity

Recognise differences in how people think, learn, communicate and experience the world while addressing genuine challenges requiring appropriate support.

O

Outcomes

Look beyond assessment numbers toward daily functioning, education, employment, relationships, confidence, wellbeing and quality of life.

V

Voice

Keep patients and families listened to, respected and meaningfully involved in decisions about care.

U

Understanding

Use assessment to create clearer understanding of needs, strengths and support strategies — not merely a diagnostic label.

S

Standards

Underpin services with evidence, specialist oversight, governance, safeguarding, supervision, audit and continuous improvement.

Governance first.The goal is not simply to grow quickly. It is to grow safely and sustainably.
Clinical foundation

Different by design.

Straightforward presentations can follow efficient evidence-based pathways, while complex or uncertain cases can access additional specialist expertise and multidisciplinary discussion.

The journey ahead

Start focused. Build comprehensively.

The launch service is focused on adult ADHD assessment. The wider vision extends into autism assessment, complex neurodevelopmental review, post-diagnostic guidance and — after the appropriate registration and clinical infrastructure are in place — prescribing, titration, monitoring and longer-term management.

Now

Assessment foundations

ADHD screening and diagnostic assessment, clear reports, appropriate onward referral and strong governance systems.

Next

Broader neurodevelopmental care

Autism pathways, complex-case review, post-diagnostic support and wider multidisciplinary collaboration as services are developed.

Future

Treatment & longer-term support

Medication initiation, titration, prescribing, monitoring, annual review and longer-term management only when the required infrastructure and regulatory arrangements are in place.

Looking beyond diagnosis

Support should reflect real life.

Where clinically appropriate, the longer-term model may include access or onward referral to evidence-based support addressing executive function, psychology, occupational needs, sleep, nutrition and lifestyle, women's health and hormonal influences, workplace adjustments, education, family relationships and community services.

Clinical boundaries matter: any holistic recommendations, referrals or future services will remain evidence-based, clinically appropriate and within clearly defined professional and regulatory boundaries.
Accessibility & inclusion

Care should be understandable in every sense.

Neurodevelopmental conditions affect people across cultures, languages and backgrounds. Novus aims over time to develop culturally informed and multilingual access where possible, using competent clinicians, professional interpreters when required and clear documentation of any linguistic or cultural limitations.

Measuring what matters

Better care should be measurable.

Where appropriate, Novus aims to collect anonymised clinical and patient-reported outcome measures to monitor quality, patient experience, longer-term effectiveness and health inequalities — and to support service improvement, future research and potential NHS partnerships.

Digital by design

Technology should remove friction, not humanity.

The planned service will use secure digital systems for online consultations, clinical records, screening questionnaires, referrals, consent, communications and outcome monitoring. Technology is intended to make care safer and easier to access while reducing unnecessary administration.

Digital, not distant.Technology supports compassionate clinical care — it does not replace it.
Wider organisation vision

A separate future metabolic health service.

The founders' longer-term strategy also explores a distinct weight-management and metabolic-health service with its own identity, governance, clinical leadership and regulatory framework. It may share selected digital and operational infrastructure with Novus while maintaining separate consent, information-governance and clinical decision-making arrangements.

Important: this is a future concept, not a current Novus Neuro Clinic service.

Our ambition is bigger than diagnosis.

We want to create specialist neurodevelopmental care that listens, measures what matters and supports people to understand their needs and move forward.
