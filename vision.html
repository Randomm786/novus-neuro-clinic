Our updated vision

Accessible neurodevelopmental care without losing depth, safety or humanity.

My Neuro Health exists to improve access to high-quality ADHD and autism care while building a service that listens, explains clearly and measures success by meaningful patient outcomes.

Our vision

We aim to establish a trusted UK neurodevelopmental service that combines specialist medical expertise, multidisciplinary care, cultural accessibility, robust governance and meaningful long-term support.

Our role is not to define people solely by a diagnosis. It is to understand individual needs, strengths and context; make assessment more accessible; and help people reach the right support.

What NEURO means to us

Our founding values are captured in the word NEURO. These are the human principles we want patients, families and clinicians to feel throughout the service.

N — Neurodiversity

People naturally think, learn, communicate and experience the world in different ways. ADHD and autism can create significant challenges requiring appropriate assessment and support, while individuals may also have valuable strengths, perspectives and abilities. We focus on the individual rather than reducing a person to a diagnosis.

E — Empathy

Seeking assessment can feel exposing, confusing or overwhelming. We aim to listen carefully, communicate with kindness and create an experience that feels calm, compassionate and human.

U — Understanding

Assessment should provide more than a diagnostic label. It should help patients, families, employers, education providers and healthcare professionals understand neurodevelopmental needs, strengths, challenges and useful support strategies.

R — Respect

We respect patient voice, individuality, culture, communication needs and informed choice. People should be meaningfully involved in decisions, and care should be done with them rather than to them.

O — Outcomes

Success should not be measured solely by how many assessments are completed. We want to understand whether care improves daily functioning, education, employment, relationships, confidence, wellbeing and quality of life.

Clinical standards sit underneath these values. Evidence-informed practice, specialist medical oversight, safeguarding, governance, multidisciplinary working, supervision, audit, information governance and continuous quality improvement remain core requirements of the service.

Phase-one service

My Neuro Health will launch with assessment and diagnosis for adult ADHD, child ADHD, adult autism and child autism. A universal triage pathway will help determine the most appropriate route and identify complexity early.

  • Adult and child ADHD assessment
  • Adult and child autism assessment
  • Combined or uncertain neurodevelopmental triage
  • Structured assessment work by appropriately trained clinicians within competence
  • Relevant ADHD or autism specialist review and diagnostic sign-off
  • Escalation of complex cases for specialist or multidisciplinary review
  • Diagnostic reports and feedback
  • GP communication with patient/parental consent where appropriate
  • Post-diagnostic guidance and appropriate onward referral

Medication initiation, titration, private prescribing and ongoing treatment are intentionally outside the initial service. They will be considered only after the appropriate CQC registration, clinical governance, indemnity, technology and monitoring infrastructure are in place.

What makes us different

Our aim is to combine an approachable patient experience with a robust clinical model. Straightforward presentations should move efficiently through standardised pathways, while diagnostic uncertainty, overlapping neurodevelopmental conditions, significant coexisting mental-health needs or other complexity should receive the level of specialist attention they require.

Governance first

Growth should be safe and sustainable. From the outset, the service is intended to develop standardised pathways, eligibility and escalation criteria, multidisciplinary review, supervision, safeguarding, information governance, complaints and incident processes, clinical audit, patient feedback, outcome monitoring and continuous improvement.

Looking beyond diagnosis

Receiving a diagnosis should not be the end of a patient journey. Where appropriate, patients may be offered information or referral relating to coaching, psychological therapies, occupational therapy, executive-function support, sleep, nutrition and lifestyle, women's health considerations, workplace or educational adjustments, family support and community services.

During phase one, external treatment and support partners remain clinically independent and make their own decisions about whether treatment or support is appropriate.

Accessibility and inclusion

Neurodevelopmental conditions affect people across every culture, language, age group and background. We aim over time to build a culturally informed and multilingual clinical network, using appropriately trained clinicians, professional interpretation where required and validated methods where available.

Measuring what matters

Where appropriate, we aim to collect clinical and patient-reported outcome measures to monitor quality, patient experience, longer-term effectiveness, inequalities, pathway performance and opportunities for improvement.

Education and advocacy

Improving neurodevelopmental care extends beyond the consultation room. We aim to engage with GPs, pharmacists, mental-health professionals, allied healthcare professionals, employers, occupational health, schools, universities, families and community organisations to improve recognition, referrals and post-diagnostic support.

Digital by design

Secure digital systems should make care easier and safer, not less human. Our longer-term model includes online consultations, secure records and portals, digital screening, efficient referrals, consent, outcome monitoring, secure professional communication and, after appropriate service expansion and registration, electronic prescribing.

Long-term ambition

Our ambition is for My Neuro Health to become one of the UK's most trusted neurodevelopmental providers — recognised for specialist medical leadership, clinical excellence, multidisciplinary expertise, cultural accessibility, strong governance, patient voice, meaningful outcomes and high professional standards.

Over time, we aim to expand beyond assessment into appropriately regulated treatment, post-diagnostic support, education and longer-term neurodevelopmental care, while retaining the warm and personal experience that defines the brand.
