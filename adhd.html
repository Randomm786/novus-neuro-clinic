ADHD assessments

ADHD assessment that looks beyond a symptom score.

For adults and children, our pathway considers developmental history, functioning across settings, mental health, strengths, challenges and the wider context of everyday life.

Adults

Adult ADHD assessment

Designed for adults who want a thorough explanation of longstanding difficulties with attention, organisation, impulsivity, restlessness or executive functioning.

  • Pre-assessment screening and developmental history
  • Functional impact across work, education, relationships and daily living
  • Mental health and differential diagnosis
  • Collateral information where available
  • Structured clinical assessment
  • Relevant specialist review and diagnostic sign-off
Children & young people

Child ADHD assessment

Children are understood in context. Parent/carer information and school evidence are important because ADHD symptoms and impairment need to be considered across settings.

  • Parent/carer developmental history
  • Age-appropriate child/young-person involvement
  • School/teacher information where appropriate
  • SEND and educational context
  • Sleep, emotional regulation and coexisting difficulties
  • Specialist review and clear feedback
Our clinical model

Efficient where straightforward. Specialist where complex.

Appropriately trained nurse or pharmacist clinicians may gather structured clinical and developmental information within their competence. The ADHD medical lead provides the relevant specialist oversight and final diagnostic pathway.

Cases involving diagnostic uncertainty, significant coexisting mental health conditions, suspected autism or other complexity are escalated early rather than treated as a routine assessment.

A combined pathway when needed

Some people show meaningful features of both ADHD and autism. If your triage suggests this, we can recommend a combined or more specialist review rather than asking you to guess which diagnosis fits first.
