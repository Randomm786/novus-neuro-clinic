# My Neuro Health — Founders' Vision v2.1

**Status:** Updated for the My Neuro Health brand, the agreed phase-one service model and the NEURO values framework.

## Our Vision
My Neuro Health exists to transform access to high-quality neurodevelopmental care by delivering clinically robust, evidence-informed ADHD and autism services that are accessible, inclusive and focused on improving long-term patient outcomes.

We aim to establish a trusted UK neurodevelopmental service that combines specialist medical expertise, multidisciplinary care, cultural accessibility, robust governance and meaningful long-term support.

## What NEURO Means to Us
Our founding values are captured in the word **NEURO**. These are the human principles we want patients, families and clinicians to experience throughout My Neuro Health.

### N — Neurodiversity
We recognise that people naturally think, learn, communicate and experience the world in different ways. ADHD and autism can create significant challenges requiring appropriate assessment and support, while individuals may also have valuable strengths, perspectives and abilities. Our role is not to reduce people to a diagnosis, but to understand the individual and help them access the right support.

### E — Empathy
Seeking assessment can feel exposing, confusing or overwhelming. We will listen carefully, communicate with kindness and aim to make the patient experience calm, compassionate and human.

### U — Understanding
Assessment should provide more than a diagnostic label. We aim to help patients, families, employers, education providers and healthcare professionals develop a clearer understanding of neurodevelopmental needs, individual strengths, challenges and useful support strategies.

### R — Respect
We respect patient voice, individuality, culture, communication needs and informed choice. Patients and families should be meaningfully involved in decisions, communication should be adapted where needed, and care should be done with people rather than to them.

### O — Outcomes
Success will not be measured solely by the number of assessments or diagnoses. We will focus on meaningful outcomes including daily functioning, education, employment, relationships, confidence, wellbeing and quality of life. Where appropriate, we will collect clinical and patient-reported outcomes to support continuous improvement.

### Clinical standards beneath the values
The NEURO values describe how the service should feel. They do not replace the clinical standards that make the service safe and credible. Every pathway will be underpinned by evidence-informed practice, specialist medical oversight, clear governance, safeguarding, multidisciplinary working, clinical supervision, audit, information governance and continuous quality improvement.

## Phase-One Service Model
My Neuro Health will launch with:
- Adult ADHD assessment and diagnosis
- Child ADHD assessment and diagnosis
- Adult autism assessment and diagnosis
- Child autism assessment and diagnosis
- Combined / uncertain neurodevelopmental triage
- Specialist review of complex presentations
- Post-diagnostic guidance
- Appropriate onward referral

The service will use one universal front-door triage process. Patients may select ADHD, autism, both, or “not sure”. Triage will identify routine, complex and urgent/safety-sensitive presentations before full assessment.

Appropriately trained nurse or pharmacist clinicians may undertake structured assessment components within competence. ADHD diagnostic decisions and sign-off will sit with Dr Amin / the ADHD Medical Lead. Autism diagnostic decisions will sit within Dr B's specialist autism pathway, with multidisciplinary involvement where clinically required. Complex or diagnostically uncertain cases will be escalated early.

Patients may receive a feedback consultation, diagnostic report, diagnosis letter, GP communication with consent, practical recommendations and appropriate referral/signposting.

Medication initiation, titration, private prescribing, ongoing medication monitoring and annual reviews will remain outside phase one. These services will be considered only after the appropriate CQC registration and implementation of the required clinical governance, indemnity, technology and monitoring systems.

## Why My Neuro Health Exists
Timely diagnosis and appropriate intervention can improve educational attainment, employment, relationships, mental wellbeing and quality of life. We want to reduce avoidable delays without reducing diagnostic quality, patient safety or the time needed to understand the person properly.

## What Makes My Neuro Health Different
Our patient-facing experience should be warm, calm and understandable. Underneath that experience, the clinical model should be specialist-led, standardised and capable of identifying complexity. Straightforward presentations should move efficiently through defined pathways, while diagnostic uncertainty, overlapping ADHD/autism, significant coexisting mental-health needs and other complexity should receive appropriate specialist or multidisciplinary review.

**Brand principle:** Human on the surface. Specialist underneath.

## A Governance-First Approach
The clinic will build the following foundations from the outset:
- Standardised patient pathways
- Evidence-informed clinical protocols
- Eligibility and exclusion criteria
- Defined clinical escalation procedures
- Multidisciplinary review and case discussion
- Clinical supervision
- Peer review and quality assurance
- Safeguarding
- Information governance
- Complaints management
- Incident reporting
- Clinical audit
- Patient feedback
- Outcome monitoring
- Continuous quality improvement

## Looking Beyond Diagnosis
A diagnosis should not be the end of a patient's journey. Where clinically appropriate, patients may benefit from access or onward referral to ADHD coaching, psychological therapies, occupational therapy, executive-function support, sleep support, nutrition and lifestyle advice, women's health considerations, workplace adjustments, educational support, family support and community services.

During phase one, external treatment/support partners remain clinically independent. They make their own assessment and treatment decisions, patient choice is preserved and information is shared only with appropriate consent.

## Accessibility and Inclusion
My Neuro Health aims over time to develop a culturally informed and multilingual clinical network. Assessments in languages other than English should use appropriately trained clinicians, validated methods where available, professional interpretation where required and clear documentation of any language/cultural limitations affecting interpretation.

## Measuring What Matters
Where appropriate, anonymised clinical and patient-reported outcome measures may be used to monitor service quality, patient experience, longer-term effectiveness, health inequalities, clinical pathways and future service development.

## Education and Advocacy
We aim to improve understanding of ADHD and autism through engagement with GPs, community pharmacists, mental-health professionals, allied healthcare professionals, employers, occupational health, schools, universities, families and community organisations.

## Digital by Design
Secure technology will support online consultations, patient records, screening questionnaires, referral pathways, consent, communications and outcome monitoring. Technology should reduce friction and improve safety without replacing compassionate clinical care.

## Future Clinical Development
Following appropriate registration and infrastructure, the ADHD pathway may expand into medication initiation, titration, private prescribing, monitoring, annual reviews, shared-care support and longer-term management.

Any wider future health service, including metabolic health, should remain a distinct clinical offering with its own identity, governance, pathways, clinical leadership and regulatory framework, even if shared digital/administrative infrastructure is used.

## Our Long-Term Ambition
Our ambition is for My Neuro Health to become one of the UK's most trusted neurodevelopmental providers, recognised for specialist medical leadership, clinical excellence, multidisciplinary expertise, cultural accessibility, robust governance, innovation, patient voice, measurable outcomes and high professional standards.

The long-term aim is comprehensive neurodevelopmental care: assessment, treatment, post-diagnostic support, education and appropriate holistic services — while preserving the warm, personal experience at the heart of the brand.
