Adult ADHD referrals

People may approach My Neuro Health directly for an adult ADHD assessment. Where professional referral information is available and the patient has consented to its use, it can contribute to the overall clinical picture.

What the patient can receive

  • Clinical feedback following assessment
  • A written diagnostic report/outcome
  • GP correspondence where appropriate and consented
  • Practical recommendations and onward options

Current treatment boundary

My Neuro Health is currently launching as an assessment service and does not provide in-house ADHD prescribing or titration at this stage.

Professional enquiry

For referral or professional questions, contact us without sending sensitive clinical information by ordinary email.
