FAQ

Referrer FAQs.

The essentials for professionals considering the pathway.

Are you currently accepting referrals?+

Not yet. Adult and child ADHD assessment services are coming soon. Referrers can register their interest and we will provide launch information when the pathway opens.

What will the Adult ADHD assessment include?+

The planned pathway includes pre-screening, a structured approximately 90-minute assessment, relevant supporting information where appropriate, clinical outcome feedback and a written report.

How quickly will reports be available?+

Our target is to make Adult ADHD reports available within 24 hours after assessment, subject to any additional information or specialist review required.

How can a professional contact My Neuro Health?+

Use the register-interest form and select the referrer option. You can also use the Email us link if you prefer to contact us directly by email.
