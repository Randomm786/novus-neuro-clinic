For referrers

A clear route for professional enquiries.

If you are a healthcare professional or another appropriate referrer and would like to discuss the suitability of an adult ADHD assessment, please contact us.

What helps us triage

  • The reason for referral and the questions you would like the assessment to address.
  • Relevant developmental, mental health and physical health history.
  • Current medication and any important risk or safeguarding information.
  • Relevant previous assessments or diagnostic reports.

Please do not send confidential patient information through an unsecured web form. Contact us first so we can confirm the appropriate route for information sharing.
