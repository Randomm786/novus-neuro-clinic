FAQ

Referrer FAQs.

The essentials for professionals considering the pathway.

Are you currently accepting referrals?+

Not yet. Adult and child ADHD assessment services are coming soon. Referrers can register their interest and we will provide launch information when the pathway opens.

What will the adult ADHD pathway include?+

The planned pathway includes pre-assessment information, a structured clinical assessment, appropriate supporting evidence, outcome feedback and a written report. Adult treatment consultations, initiation, titration and ongoing care are also being developed. Treatment is subject to individual suitability and is not automatic.

View the planned adult treatment pathway.

When will reports be available?+

We will confirm the expected report turnaround before bookings or referrals open. Reports will be issued after the clinician has reviewed the required information and completed appropriate checks.

How can a professional contact My Neuro Health?+

Use the register-interest form and select the referrer option. You can also use the Email us link if you prefer to contact us directly by email.

Will you request shared care with a GP?+

Shared care may form part of a suitable ongoing-care plan, but requires agreement and must not be assumed. Our referral guidance will explain prescribing responsibilities, follow-up and what happens if shared care is not accepted.
