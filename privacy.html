Privacy

Privacy should be clear, not buried.

This page is a pre-launch website privacy draft and should be reviewed against the final legal entity, patient system and supplier setup before publication.

Website enquiries

The current prototype does not submit or store enquiry-form information. The contact form opens your own email application. Once a secure enquiry service is connected, this notice will be updated to explain the data controller, lawful bases, processors, retention and your rights.

Clinical information

Health information and assessment documentation should only be collected through the clinic's approved secure clinical systems, with role-based access and appropriate information-governance controls. Do not send detailed medical records through a general website contact form.

Analytics and cookies

No analytics platform is included in this build. If analytics, advertising pixels or non-essential cookies are added, an appropriate cookie mechanism and updated privacy information should be implemented before launch.
