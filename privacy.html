Privacy

Privacy notice

This website is designed to minimise the collection of personal information. We do not currently use an online medical-history form on the public website.

Registering interest

The register-interest form prepares a draft in your own email application. You must send that draft to complete an enquiry. The website has no form-submission server or database. If you send the email, the information is received in our mailbox so that we can respond. Please do not include detailed medical information in a general enquiry.

Website analytics

Our custom analytics events do not include your name, email address, phone number, message or selected enquiry category. An email-draft event records an attempt to open an email app, not delivery of an enquiry. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising, profiling, Google Signals or user-provided data.

Your rights

UK data-protection law provides rights in relation to personal information. This notice will be expanded with full controller details, lawful bases, retention periods, recipients and clinical contact routes before clinical data-collection workflows go live.
