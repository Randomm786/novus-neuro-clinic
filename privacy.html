Privacy

Privacy notice

This website is designed to minimise the collection of personal information. We do not currently use an online medical-history form on the public website.

Contacting us

If you contact My Neuro Health by email, we will receive the information you choose to provide so that we can respond to your enquiry. Please avoid sending detailed or highly sensitive medical information until we confirm an appropriate secure route.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics, provided by Google, to understand how visitors use our website and to help us improve it. Google Analytics is only activated when you choose to accept analytics. It may process information such as pages visited, referral source, device and browser information, and interactions with the website. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising, profiling, Google Signals or user-provided data. You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

Your rights

UK data-protection law provides rights in relation to personal information. This notice will be expanded with full controller details, lawful bases, retention periods, recipients and contact routes before any clinical data-collection workflows go live.
