# My Neuro Health — Phase 2 Brand Direction

## Positioning
**Human on the surface. Specialist underneath.**

## Hero
**Understand yourself. Understand what comes next.**

## Current service scope
Adult ADHD assessment is live. Child ADHD is Coming Soon. Autism is removed from the public site for now.

## NEURO values
- **N — Neurodiversity:** Every mind is different.
- **E — Empathy:** We listen before we assess.
- **U — Understanding:** A diagnosis should bring clarity, not just a label.
- **R — Respect:** Your voice and experience matter.
- **O — Outcomes:** What happens after assessment matters too.

## Visual language
Warm white and cream surfaces, olive and sage accents, deep ink typography, human/coastal photography, generous white space, minimal botanical detail.

The olive motif represents peace, rootedness, resilience, growth and hope. No political/geographic explanation is included in public copy at this stage.
