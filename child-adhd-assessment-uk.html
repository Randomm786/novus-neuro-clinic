Understanding the whole picture

Children can show difficulties with attention, activity levels, impulsivity, organisation or emotional regulation in very different ways. Similar difficulties can also occur for reasons other than ADHD.

A child ADHD assessment therefore needs to consider development over time, the impact on everyday functioning, what happens across different settings and whether there are other factors that may better explain the child's needs.

Information from home and education

Parents or carers are an important part of the assessment. Where appropriate, information from school or another education setting may also help the clinician understand how the child manages in different environments.

The exact process will depend on age and individual circumstances. We will publish the full pathway before appointments open.

FAQ

Child ADHD assessment FAQs

When will child ADHD assessments launch?+

The child ADHD assessment pathway is currently coming soon. Parents and carers can register interest for launch updates.

What information may be needed for a child ADHD assessment?+

A clinician may need information about the child's experiences at home and in education, developmental history, health, behaviour and functioning. Questionnaires or information from school may also be requested where appropriate.

Does an assessment guarantee an ADHD diagnosis?+

No. The purpose of assessment is to understand the child's needs and reach the most appropriate clinical conclusion from the available information.

Will the child's school be involved?+

Educational information can be valuable because ADHD symptoms are considered across settings. The exact information requested will depend on the child, age and circumstances.

Will pricing be shown before launch?+

Yes. Final private assessment pricing will be published clearly before appointments open.

Does the adult treatment pathway apply to children?+

No. Our adult initiation and titration information describes a separate service. We will explain the scope and age-appropriate arrangements for the child pathway before bookings open, including what support may be available after assessment.
