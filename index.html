FAQ

Questions people usually ask first.

Short, clear answers about the services we are preparing.

Are Adult ADHD assessments available now?+

Not yet. Adult ADHD assessments are coming soon. You can register your interest now and we will contact you when appointments open.

Are Child ADHD assessments available now?+

Not yet. Our child ADHD assessment pathway is also coming soon, and you can register your interest.

How quickly will I be able to get an Adult ADHD appointment?+

Once the service is live, our aim is to offer an appointment within 24 hours of a suitable enquiry, subject to clinician availability.

How long will the Adult ADHD assessment take?+

The planned Adult ADHD assessment appointment is approximately 90 minutes.

Is there a pre-screen before the assessment?+

Yes. A pre-screening stage will be available before the full Adult ADHD assessment to help us understand your needs and the suitability of the pathway.

How quickly will I receive my report?+

Our aim is to make the Adult ADHD assessment report available within 24 hours after the assessment, subject to any additional information or clinical review that may be needed.
