What NEURO means to us

Five values that shape how we care.

NEURO is more than part of our name. It is a simple reminder of how we want every person to experience My Neuro Health: understood, respected and supported by care that looks beyond a label.

N
Neurodiversity

Every mind is different. We recognise individual ways of thinking, communicating and experiencing the world.

E
Empathy

We listen before we assess, and aim to make every interaction calm, kind and human.

U
Understanding

A diagnosis should bring clarity — helping you make sense of strengths, challenges, needs and what comes next.

R
Respect

Your voice, identity, culture, communication needs and choices matter. Care should be done with you, not to you.

O
Outcomes

What happens after assessment matters too: confidence, relationships, education, work, wellbeing and quality of life.

After diagnosis

The assessment is not the end of the journey.

Where appropriate, your outcome can include a diagnostic report, a letter for your GP with your consent, practical recommendations and discussion of onward support.

During our assessment-only launch phase, treatment remains separate. We may signpost or refer to independent partners for medication or holistic support where appropriate. Future in-house ADHD treatment is planned only after the appropriate regulatory registration, governance and clinical infrastructure are in place.
