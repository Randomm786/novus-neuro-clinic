FAQ

Questions people usually ask first.

Short, clear answers about the services we are preparing.

Are Adult ADHD assessments available now?+

Not yet. Adult ADHD assessments are coming soon. You can register your interest now and we will contact you when appointments open.

Are Child ADHD assessments available now?+

Not yet. Our child ADHD assessment pathway is also coming soon, and you can register your interest.

When can I book an adult ADHD appointment?+

Appointments are not open yet. We will publish launch dates and availability once the service is ready. Registering interest means you can receive updates; it does not reserve an appointment or a place in a clinical waiting list.

How long will the adult ADHD assessment take?+

We will confirm the appointment length, preparation and any follow-up arrangements before you book. The process will allow for the information and discussion needed for your individual assessment.

Is there a pre-screen before the assessment?+

Yes. A pre-screening stage will be available before the full Adult ADHD assessment to help us understand your needs and the suitability of the pathway.

When will I receive my report?+

We will confirm the expected report turnaround before you book. Reports will follow the required clinical review, and we will explain when additional information or checks are needed.

Will there be support after an adult ADHD diagnosis?+

Yes. Our planned adult pathway includes treatment discussions, follow-up and ongoing care where appropriate. Availability and fees will be confirmed before bookings open. Explore the planned adult treatment service.
