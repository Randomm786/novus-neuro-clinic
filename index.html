The pathway

Designed to make the process easier to understand.

Our launch model is intentionally straightforward. We want patients to know what happens next, what information is needed, and what the possible outcomes of an assessment may be.

01

Initial information

Complete your history, screening information and relevant background before the clinical assessment.

02

Clinical assessment

A structured appointment explores symptoms, functional impact, history and relevant differential considerations.

03

Clinical review

Your information is reviewed carefully before an outcome is communicated. An ADHD diagnosis is never guaranteed.

04

Report & next steps

Where appropriate, you receive a written clinical outcome with recommendations for what may come next.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does an assessment guarantee an ADHD diagnosis?

No. The purpose of an assessment is to reach an appropriate clinical outcome based on the information available. A particular diagnosis is never guaranteed.

Will appointments be online?

Novus Neuro Clinic is being developed as an online-first service for UK adults. The exact pathway and any requirements for additional information will be confirmed before booking.

Do you prescribe ADHD medication?

The initial website is focused on the assessment service. Any treatment, prescribing or titration service will only be advertised once the relevant clinical and regulatory arrangements are in place.

When will bookings open?

We are currently completing the service setup. You can register your interest and we will publish launch information once the pathway is ready.

Interested in an assessment?

Register your interest and we can contact you when Novus Neuro Clinic opens bookings.
