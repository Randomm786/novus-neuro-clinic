Common questions

Before you decide.

Does an assessment guarantee an ADHD diagnosis?

No. The purpose of an assessment is to reach the most appropriate clinical outcome based on the information available.

Will appointments be online?

The launch service is being developed as an online-first pathway for UK adults. Final requirements will be published before booking.

Will Novus prescribe ADHD medication?

The launch website is focused on assessment. Treatment, prescribing or titration will only be advertised once the relevant clinical and regulatory arrangements are finalised.

Will Novus also offer autism assessments?

Autism screening and diagnostic assessment form part of the wider Novus vision. The initial launch focus is adult ADHD assessment, and any additional services will only open once the relevant pathways, clinicians and governance arrangements are ready.

When will bookings open?

Service setup is underway. Register your interest and we will publish launch information once the pathway is ready.
