About My Neuro Health

Understanding should change what comes next.

We are building an ADHD service that connects careful assessment with thoughtful next steps, including a planned adult treatment pathway. Clinically considered and genuinely human.

Our vision

We want people to leave assessment with more than an answer. The aim is clearer understanding of strengths, difficulties, needs and what may help next.

Human on the surface. Specialist underneath.

The public experience should feel calm, respectful and easy to navigate. Underneath that sits structured assessment, appropriate clinical review, safeguarding, information governance and clear accountability.

Current status: Adult ADHD assessment and treatment, and child ADHD assessment, are in preparation. Register interest for updates; appointments are not yet open.

About the company

My Neuro Health is the trading identity of My Neuro Health Ltd, a UK company developing neurodevelopmental assessment and care services. Our public information will be updated as services, clinicians and regulatory arrangements are finalised.

What we are preparing

Adult ADHD assessment
Clinical assessment, feedback and written documentation.

Adult treatment and follow-up
Medication initiation, titration and ongoing care where appropriate.

Child ADHD assessment
A separate pathway being developed around children and families.

All services are coming soon. Full scope, eligibility and availability will be published before bookings open.
