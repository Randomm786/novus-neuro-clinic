About Novus

A new approach, built on strong clinical foundations.

Novus Neuro Clinic is being developed as a modern UK neurodevelopmental service with adult ADHD assessment as its first clinical pathway.

What Novus means

Novus comes from Latin and conveys the idea of something new or renewed. For us, it represents a clearer way to approach neurodevelopmental care.

How we are building the service

The clinic is being developed by healthcare professionals with experience across clinical service design, governance, safeguarding, information governance and regulated healthcare operations.

Our priorities

  • Clinical quality before rapid growth.
  • Respectful, individualised care.
  • Clear patient communication.
  • Strong governance and documentation.
  • Honest expectations about assessment outcomes.
