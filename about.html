About My Neuro Health

Understanding should change what comes next.

We are building a specialist ADHD assessment service that feels clinically rigorous and genuinely human.

Our vision

We want people to leave assessment with more than an answer. The aim is clearer understanding of strengths, difficulties, needs and what may help next.

Human on the surface. Specialist underneath.

The public experience should feel calm, respectful and easy to navigate. Underneath that sits structured assessment, appropriate clinical review, safeguarding, information governance and clear accountability.
