About My Neuro Health

Understanding should change what comes next.

My Neuro Health was created around a simple idea: neurodevelopmental assessment should feel clinically rigorous and genuinely human at the same time.

Our vision

We want people to leave assessment with more than an answer. We want them to have a clearer understanding of themselves, their strengths, their needs and the options available to them.

Our values

NEURO stands for Neurodiversity, Empathy, Understanding, Respect and Outcomes. These values guide how we communicate, assess, make decisions and think about what happens after an assessment.

Warm on the surface. Specialist underneath.

Our public experience should feel calm, accessible and respectful. Underneath that sits structured triage, clinical accountability, appropriate escalation, information governance, safeguarding and continuous quality improvement.

What we offer now

Adult ADHD assessment is our current launch service.

Child ADHD assessment is coming soon.

Other neurodevelopmental services are intentionally not being advertised at this stage.
