About us

We are building the kind of neurodevelopmental service we would want to use ourselves.

Warm enough to feel approachable. Thorough enough to stand up clinically. Specialist enough to manage complexity without making every person feel like a complex case.

Why My Neuro Health

Better access should not mean lowering the clinical bar.

Long waits can delay understanding, adjustments and support. But speed alone is not a good outcome if the assessment feels rushed, generic or difficult to defend.

My Neuro Health is designed around a different balance: clear digital pathways and trained multidisciplinary clinicians for efficiency, with specialist medical leadership, escalation and quality assurance underpinning the service.

Personal, thorough and specialist-led.That is the standard we want patients to feel — and clinicians to recognise.
Clinical leadership

Different expertise for different questions.

Our intended launch model separates ADHD and autism specialist oversight while allowing appropriately trained multidisciplinary clinicians to support structured assessment work.

AD

ADHD Medical Lead

Dr Amin leads the ADHD diagnostic standard, specialist review and clinical sign-off pathway.

AU

Autism Specialist Lead

Dr B leads the autism specialist pathway and the level of multidisciplinary involvement required for each case.

MD

Multidisciplinary clinicians

Appropriately trained nurses, pharmacists and other clinicians contribute within defined competencies, supervision and governance.
