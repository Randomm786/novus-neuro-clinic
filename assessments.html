ADHD assessments

A structured route to better understanding.

Our planned adult ADHD assessment pathway is designed to gather a detailed clinical picture, consider relevant alternatives and provide a clear outcome without promising a particular diagnosis.

What the assessment may include

  • Developmental and symptom history.
  • Impact on work, education, relationships and day-to-day functioning.
  • Relevant physical and mental health history.
  • Structured clinical questioning and validated assessment tools where appropriate.
  • Consideration of alternative or co-existing explanations.
  • Clinical review and written outcome.

What it is not

An ADHD assessment is not a guaranteed route to diagnosis or medication. The clinician must reach an outcome based on the information available and their professional judgement.
