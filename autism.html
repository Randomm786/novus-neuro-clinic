Autism assessments

An autism assessment should help make sense of a life — not just produce a label.

Our autism pathway is designed around developmental history, communication, sensory experience, routines, relationships, strengths and the ways people may adapt or mask over time.

Adults

Adult autism assessment

A calm, respectful assessment for adults who may have spent years feeling different, adapting socially or wondering whether autism helps explain their experiences.

  • Developmental and social history
  • Communication and relationship patterns
  • Routines, interests and sensory profile
  • Masking and compensatory strategies
  • Mental health and overlapping ADHD features
  • Specialist autism review
Children & young people

Child autism assessment

For children, good assessment brings together the child or young person's presentation with developmental, family, school and other relevant information rather than relying on one questionnaire alone.

  • Parent/carer developmental history
  • Age-appropriate observation and interaction
  • School/nursery information where appropriate
  • Language, sensory and developmental context
  • Consideration of coexisting conditions
  • Specialist autism team input as required

Different people need different assessment routes.

Autism is not a single presentation. Age, communication needs, developmental history, learning needs, masking and coexisting conditions can all change what a high-quality assessment should involve.

Specialist-led

One pathway should not be forced onto everyone.

Structured information gathering may be completed by appropriately trained clinicians, but autism diagnostic decisions sit within the specialist autism pathway. Where complexity requires broader multidisciplinary input, the case is escalated rather than simply “signed off” after a generic assessment.

Not sure whether ADHD or autism fits? You do not need to choose perfectly before contacting us. Our initial neurodevelopmental triage can help identify whether one or both pathways should be considered.
