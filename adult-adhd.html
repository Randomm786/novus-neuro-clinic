Adult ADHD

Adult ADHD assessment, with the whole person in view.

A comprehensive assessment designed to explore whether ADHD may help explain your experiences, difficulties and strengths — and to give you clear next steps.

What the assessment looks at

An ADHD assessment is more than a symptom checklist. The clinical process may explore your developmental history, current symptoms, day-to-day functioning, education or work, relationships, mental health, physical health and other factors that may offer an alternative or additional explanation.

What you can expect

  • Initial screening and triage before the main assessment.
  • A structured clinical assessment with an appropriately trained clinician.
  • Collateral or supporting information where clinically appropriate.
  • Specialist review and escalation where the presentation is complex or uncertain.
  • Clear feedback on the outcome and what happens next.
  • A written diagnostic report or outcome letter.

After the assessment

If ADHD is diagnosed, we will explain relevant next-step options. My Neuro Health is currently launching as an assessment-focused service and does not provide in-house ADHD medication initiation or titration at this stage.

Important: An ADHD diagnosis is not guaranteed. The outcome is based on the clinical assessment and the information available.
