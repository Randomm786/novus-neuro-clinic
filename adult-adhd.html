FAQ

Adult ADHD assessment FAQs.

The detail people usually want before deciding whether to register interest.

When will Adult ADHD assessments launch?+

Adult ADHD assessments are currently coming soon. You can register your interest now so we can contact you when appointments open.

How long is the assessment?+

The planned Adult ADHD assessment appointment is approximately 90 minutes.

How quickly can I get an appointment?+

Once live, our aim is to offer an appointment within 24 hours of a suitable enquiry, subject to clinician availability.

Is there a pre-screen?+

Yes. A pre-screening stage will be available before the full assessment to help us understand your needs and whether the pathway appears suitable.

What does the assessment consider?+

The assessment is designed to consider symptoms, developmental history, day-to-day functioning and other relevant factors rather than relying on a checklist alone.

Will I definitely receive an ADHD diagnosis?+

No. A diagnosis is not guaranteed. The outcome depends on the clinical assessment and the information available.

When will I receive my report?+

Our aim is to make the report available within 24 hours after the assessment, subject to any additional information or specialist review that may be required.

Does My Neuro Health currently provide ADHD medication?+

No. The current planned launch is assessment-focused. In-house medication initiation and titration are not currently offered.
