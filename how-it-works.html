How it works

From understanding to the next right step.

Our adult service is coming soon. We are preparing a pathway that connects assessment with personalised treatment planning and follow-up, where appropriate. Child assessment arrangements will be published separately.

1

Register interest

Choose the service you are interested in. We will share launch information and explain how to enquire once appointments open.

2

Pre-assessment information

We will explain what information is needed to understand your concerns and consider whether the service is suitable.

3

Assessment and feedback

A clinical assessment considers the wider picture. We plan to explain the outcome and provide a written report; diagnosis is not guaranteed.

4

Discuss treatment options

Where relevant, a separate consultation considers your goals, suitability for treatment, required checks and the available options.

5

Initiation and titration

Where medication is appropriate and agreed, our planned service includes starting treatment and follow-up while the plan is adjusted.

6

Ongoing care

We will explain continuing reviews, prescribing responsibilities and costs, including any proposed shared-care arrangements.

Not everyone follows every step.

The outcome might be ADHD, a different explanation, or the need for further assessment. Treatment may involve practical or non-medication support rather than prescribing. The aim is an appropriate next step, not a predetermined outcome.

Complex or uncertain presentations may need additional information or another specialist service. We will explain the next steps and any additional costs before you decide whether to proceed.
