Patient journey

Know what happens before you begin.

We are designing the Novus pathway so that patients understand each stage before committing to an assessment.

1. Register your interest

Tell us that you would like to hear when bookings open. No payment is taken at this stage.

2. Pre-assessment information

Patients will be asked to provide relevant history and screening information so that the clinical appointment can be used effectively.

3. Clinical assessment

A clinician explores current symptoms, developmental history, functional impact and relevant alternative or co-existing conditions.

4. Review and outcome

The information is reviewed and an appropriate clinical outcome is communicated. Further information may sometimes be required before a conclusion can be reached.

5. Report and recommendations

Where appropriate, a written report and next-step recommendations are provided. Any future treatment service will be described separately once available.
