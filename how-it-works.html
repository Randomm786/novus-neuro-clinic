How it works

A patient journey designed to make sense.

From first contact through to your report, each stage is being built around clear expectations and responsible clinical decision-making.

01

Register

Join the launch list and receive information once bookings open.

02

Prepare

Complete requested history and screening information ahead of the appointment.

03

Meet

Attend your structured online clinical assessment.

04

Review

The information is considered carefully before a clinical outcome is reached.

05

Move forward

Receive your outcome, report and appropriate next-step recommendations.

Designed around clarity

You should know what happens next.

We want to remove avoidable uncertainty from the administrative side of the assessment so the clinical conversation can stay focused on what matters.

Before

Clear instructions on what information is needed and why.

During

A structured assessment with space for your individual context.

After

A clear explanation of the clinical outcome and what it means.

Next

Recommendations matched to the outcome rather than a one-size-fits-all route.

What happens behind the scenes

Clinical quality is part of the pathway too.

The planned service model includes documented pathways, escalation criteria, safeguarding, peer review, clinical supervision, patient feedback and continuous quality improvement. Where a case is more complex, the journey may include further information gathering or specialist discussion before an outcome is finalised.

V

Patient voice

Communication and consent should be adapted to the person, not the other way around.

S

Standards

Clear governance and professional accountability underpin the clinical process.

O

Outcomes

Over time, Novus aims to measure patient experience and meaningful longer-term outcomes.
