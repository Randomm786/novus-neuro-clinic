How it works

One clear pathway, adapted to the person in front of us.

Our process is designed to catch complexity early, collect the right information before the main assessment and make the final outcome easier to understand.

Triage first

We try to put you on the right pathway before the full assessment.

Our intake asks about what has brought you to assessment, developmental history, current functioning, previous diagnoses, mental health, safety and the availability of supporting information.

That information is used to decide whether the case is suitable for a routine pathway, needs specialist review before booking, or should be paused while an urgent clinical or safeguarding issue is addressed.

Examples of reasons for specialist review

Possible ADHD and autism together, major diagnostic uncertainty, significant trauma, psychosis or bipolar history, complex substance use, major developmental or learning needs, conflicting evidence, complex safeguarding circumstances or previous inconclusive assessment.

What to prepare

  • GP details
  • Any previous ADHD/autism or mental-health reports
  • School reports, EHCP/SEND information where relevant
  • Developmental information from someone who knew you/your child earlier in life where possible
  • Current medication and relevant health information
  • Questions you want answered by the assessment

Possible outcomes

  • Diagnostic criteria met
  • Diagnostic criteria not met
  • Further information needed
  • An alternative/additional condition should be considered
  • Diagnostic uncertainty requiring further specialist or multidisciplinary review
After the outcome

Clear explanation matters as much as the label.

Where appropriate, you receive a diagnostic report, a GP letter with consent, practical recommendations and discussion of suitable next-step support. During phase one, medication treatment is provided only by separate independent services if you choose to pursue it.
