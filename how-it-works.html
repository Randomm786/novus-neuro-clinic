How it works

A clear process from first enquiry to next steps.

We want the route into assessment to feel understandable and proportionate from the outset.

1

Enquiry

Tell us what you are looking for and any important background information.

2

Triage

We review whether the pathway appears suitable and identify anything that needs specialist input early.

3

Assessment

The clinician completes the structured assessment and gathers relevant supporting information.

4

Outcome

You receive feedback, written documentation and clear next steps based on the clinical outcome.

Complex or uncertain presentations

Some people have overlapping symptoms, co-existing conditions or histories that make a straightforward pathway inappropriate. Those cases should be identified at triage or during assessment and escalated for appropriate specialist review rather than forced through a one-size-fits-all process.

Assessment first

Our current service is focused on assessment and diagnostic clarification. Medication treatment is planned as a later phase only when the appropriate regulatory, clinical and operational infrastructure is in place.

Personal, thorough and specialist-led ADHD assessment, centred on the whole person.
